LEO, home assistant for the elderly

A webcam-based fall-detection and care assistant, built as a final year project in a team of three. I led the computer vision design and owned the detection pipeline, backend and conversational AI.

A six-signal verification step (balance, outline, blur, posture, trajectory, pose) and caregiver-set safe zones cut false alarms. Result: 94% recall, 98% specificity, 93.1% F1, alerts in under 2 seconds, and a 4.4/5 satisfaction rating from elderly participants.PyTorch, YOLO, OpenCV, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, QwenView on GitHub (external)
